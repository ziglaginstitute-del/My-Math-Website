Our Origins

Born from a Passion to Cure Mathematical Phobia

Ziglag Institute was birthed in Lagos, Nigeria, by Mr. Isaac Olusola, an Applied Mathematician who witnessed firsthand how brilliant young minds were prematurely locked out of medicine, engineering, computer science, and economics simply because of poor, uninspired mathematical instruction.

Traditional classrooms too often relied on intimidation, mechanical memorization, and shortcuts without substance. Isaac began hosting whiteboard breakdown sessions, proving that with visual clarity, patient derivations, and structured past-question calibration, even students who previously scored below 30% could achieve distinctions above 85% in WAEC and 90+ in JAMB Mathematics.

🏛️

From a Whiteboard to a Full Digital Academy

Today, Ziglag Institute encompasses an interactive course video library, the PrepCBT timed simulator, an open-access past question vault, and live one-on-one digital coaching.
